What is AgoraAI
The smartest room
in the building.

AgoraAI is a live co-design intelligence platform built for teams that want more from their workshops, meetings, and strategy sessions. We believe the best ideas already exist in the room — they just need the right tools to surface them.

Named after the ancient Greek agora — the public gathering place where ideas were debated, decisions were made, and communities were built — AgoraAI brings that same energy into the modern workplace, powered by AI.

The Problem
Most workshops waste
most of the room.

Traditional brainstorming tools collect input but leave the hard work — analysing, synthesising, finding patterns — to someone after the session. By then, the energy is gone and the nuance is lost.

AgoraAI changes that. As participants respond, AI works in real time to cluster similar ideas, identify dominant themes, surface standout insights, and back them with context — all while the conversation is still live.

How it works
Up and running
in under 2 minutes.
01
Create your session
Give your session a title and add your questions — one per slide. Choose between Idea Clusters, Theme Ranking, or Sentiment for each question. Hit Launch and you're live.
02
Share the code or QR
A 6-character room code stays pinned at the bottom of your screen throughout the session. Share it verbally, copy the link, or show the QR code — participants join instantly on any device.
03
Watch insights build live
As responses come in, your Insights Dashboard updates automatically. AI clusters similar ideas, reads sentiment, surfaces standout responses, and writes a synthesis — all in real time, no button needed.
04
Advance through questions
Move through your slides with Prev and Next. Participants' screens update automatically — they always see the current question. No new links, no new codes, no friction.
05
End with a deliverable
Click End Session for a full AI synthesis across all your questions — strongest themes, key tensions, actionable insight, and a recommended next step. Copy it, share it, act on it.
