What is AgoraAI
The smartest room
in the building.

AgoraAI is a live co-design intelligence platform built for teams that want more from their workshops, meetings, and strategy sessions. We believe the best ideas already exist in the room — they just need the right tools to surface them.

Named after the ancient Greek agora — the public gathering place where ideas were debated, decisions were made, and communities were built — AgoraAI brings that same energy into the modern workplace, powered by AI.

The Problem
Most workshops waste
most of the room.

Traditional brainstorming tools collect input but leave the hard work — analysing, synthesising, finding patterns — to someone after the session. By then, the energy is gone and the nuance is lost.

AgoraAI changes that. As participants respond, AI works in real time to cluster similar ideas, identify dominant themes, surface standout insights, and back them with context — all while the conversation is still live.

How it works
Up and running
in under 2 minutes.
1
Create your session
Click "Host a Session" and give your session a title. Add your questions — one per slide. Choose between Idea Clusters, Theme Ranking, or Sentiment for each question depending on what kind of response you want.
2
Share the room code or QR
Once your session launches, a 6-character room code appears at the bottom of the screen — always visible during your presentation. Share it verbally, copy the link, or show the QR code on your screen for instant joining.
3
Watch the insights build
As participants respond, your Insights Dashboard updates in real time. The AI automatically clusters responses, identifies top themes, and generates a synthesis — no button needed. When you're ready for deeper analysis, hit "AI Insight" for a web-backed commentary.
4
Advance through your questions
Use the Prev and Next buttons to move through your slides. Participants' screens update automatically — they always see the current question. No need to share new links or codes.
5
End with a summary
When you're done, click "End Session" to receive a full AI-generated synthesis of everything discussed across all your questions. Copy it for your notes, share it with the team, or use it as a launchpad for next steps.
1
Join the session
Click "Join a Session" and enter the 6-character code shown by your host. Or scan the QR code if it's displayed. No account, no app download — just tap and you're in.
2
Answer honestly
You'll see the host's question on your screen. Type your response and hit Submit. All responses are anonymous — your name is never shown to the host or other participants. Say what you really think.
3
Follow along
When the host moves to the next question, your screen updates automatically. You'll see a progress indicator showing where you are in the session. You can submit multiple responses to any question.
FAQ
Common questions.
Is AgoraAI free to use? ▾
AgoraAI is currently in early access and free to use. We're building the product in public and welcome feedback from early users. Pricing will be introduced as the platform grows, with a generous free tier always available for small teams.
Do participants need to create an account? ▾
No. Participants join via a link or room code — no account, no app download, no friction. They just enter the code and start responding. We believe the lowest possible barrier to participation leads to the best sessions.
Are responses anonymous? ▾
Yes, completely. Participant names are never collected or shown to the host. We believe anonymity leads to more honest, higher-quality responses — especially in a corporate setting where people may feel inhibited.
How many participants can join a session? ▾
AgoraAI is built to scale. There's no hard cap — sessions work equally well for 5 people or 500. The AI clustering becomes more powerful with more responses, so larger groups actually produce richer insights.
How does the AI clustering work? ▾
AgoraAI uses Claude (Anthropic's AI) to semantically group responses — meaning it understands meaning, not just matching words. "We need more training" and "upskilling the team is critical" would be grouped together even though they use different words.
Can I save or export my session results? ▾
At the end of every session, AgoraAI generates a full AI synthesis you can copy to your clipboard. Past sessions are saved in your session history. Full export functionality (PDF, CSV) is on our roadmap.
What kinds of sessions work best with AgoraAI? ▾
AgoraAI shines in any setting where you want to quickly understand what a group thinks: strategy workshops, client discovery sessions, team retrospectives, town halls, training sessions, conference panels, and stakeholder consultations. If you need to synthesise group thinking fast, AgoraAI is built for you.
Is my data secure? ▾
Session data is stored securely via Supabase (PostgreSQL) with industry-standard encryption. We do not sell data to third parties. AI analysis is performed via Anthropic's API, which is subject to Anthropic's privacy and data policies.
